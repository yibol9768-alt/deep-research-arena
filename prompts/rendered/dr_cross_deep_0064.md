online:

I cook most nights and my knife skills have finally outgrown the block set from college. Chef's knives run from thirty bucks to three hundred and beyond, and I can't figure out where the real jump is. Does harder steel or forged construction actually change how it cuts and how long the edge lasts, or does everything past some point just buy a nicer handle and a name? Long-time owners must know which cheap knives embarrassed them and which expensive ones weren't worth it. I can afford the middle, maybe more. Where's the cutoff, and what would you buy at it?
