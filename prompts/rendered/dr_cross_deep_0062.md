I've been eyeing those gorgeous Damascus-looking chef's knives that sell for way under a hundred and sit at nearly five stars. But whenever knife people talk about them, someone sneers that the wavy pattern is just laser-etched onto soft steel and the edge folds in a month. So who's right, the thousands of happy buyers or the snobs? I'd love to understand what real Damascus or layered steel even is, whether it matters for how a knife holds an edge, and whether any of these pretty affordable knives are secretly fine. If not, what should my money go to instead?
