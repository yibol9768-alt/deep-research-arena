## The request

There's a budget mechanical keyboard sitting at nearly five stars with thousands of ratings, half the price of anything the enthusiasts recommend. But whenever the brand comes up among hobbyists, people groan about keys double-typing after a few months and warranty runaround. Are those star ratings just honeymoon-period impressions from people who never had a keyboard fail yet, or is the hate pure snobbery? I type for a living, so a board that develops chatter would genuinely cost me. I'd love to know what actually causes that double-typing, whether it's fixable, and what I should buy if the fears are founded.
