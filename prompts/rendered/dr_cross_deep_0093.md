You have a search tool that reaches three sources, and nothing else is
online:

- an online store with product pages (names, prices, ratings, reviews),
- a discussion forum with threads, comments, and vote counts,
- an encyclopedia with reference articles.

## The request

I've decided I want an electric sit-stand desk, but the price spread is baffling, some are around $170 and the well-known ones push $600 or more, and the listings all read the same: dual motor, steel frame, memory presets. My desk will hold two monitors and I type standing up, so wobble worries me. What does the extra money physically buy inside these things, and at what price does the improvement actually stop mattering for a normal home office? Long-term owners seem to have strong opinions about which frames go shaky or fail. Tell me where the sensible cutoff is and which desk you'd put your own money on.
