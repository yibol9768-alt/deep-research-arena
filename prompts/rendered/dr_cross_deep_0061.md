## Sandbox preamble (identical for all agents)

You have a search tool that reaches three sources, and nothing else is
online:

- an online store with product pages (names, prices, ratings, reviews),
- a discussion forum with threads, comments, and vote counts,
- an encyclopedia with reference articles.

When you use a page, link to it inline so the reader can check it. Only cite a
page if it genuinely backs what you say next to the link.

## The request

My omelet pan started shedding black flecks into breakfast this week, two years in, so I'm shopping for a replacement with serious trust issues. The candidates all lean on ceramic, PFOA-free, diamond-infused, granite coating, and metal-utensil safe, and I half suspect it's the same chemistry under a fresh sticker. What are these coatings actually made of, and does any of that change how fast a pan loses its slick? Did buyers of the fancier versions watch them flake on the same schedule as mine did? Then tell me plainly which pan you'd cook on every morning.
