公开问题

I am clearing out a family drawer of chargers and cables. Most of the newer gear has the same reversible USB-C plug, yet one phone is slow on one brick, similar-looking cables do different things, and adding a second device can change the result. Please explain how USB connections converged on USB-C without making every function identical, and do not assume that matching USB-C connectors have matching capabilities. Separate connector, protocol, charger port, phone profile, cable, and device state. Show how phone-supported profiles and proprietary charging change the result, but keep each phone example at its exact model and charging method.

Use the available ULTRANET and ALOGIC listings and the two different USB-C cable listings as concrete examples. Treat product-page numbers as seller claims with their stated port and cable conditions. Compare each charger port and each cable at its stated power, data, and sharing conditions, including the one-device versus multiport allocations, the claimed cable power and data rates, e-marker status, and any video condition. Relate those claims to USB negotiation, the Galaxy S22 family profiles, and the Realme GT Neo5 bundle example. Do not promise the fastest mode for every compatible combination.

Audit the phone-owner threads in a compact table. Separate charge state, port debris, repair history, and connector type in the owner reports, and record the phone model, MagSafe or wired method, observed outcome, and unresolved confounders. Keep Lightning experiences separate from USB-C cable durability, and do not turn individual forum experiences into a general reliability ranking. Identify which claims are supported and which require direct testing.

Build a phone-brick-port-cable matrix for the family. For every combination, say which phone documentation, charger-port profile and occupancy, and cable power, current, data-generation, e-marker, or alternate-mode fields should be recorded. Give a controlled procedure that holds starting battery range, temperature, phone activity, elapsed time, and other-port use steady, then logs negotiated mode when observable, battery change, measured power when suitable equipment is available, temperature, disconnects, data or video behavior, and the effect of adding another device. Finish with a practical per-phone compatibility table and test plan, and make conditional purchase choices without naming a universal winner. State clearly what a successful test of one exact combination can establish and what remains unknown about broad speed and long-term reliability.
