USB began as a way to standardize peripheral connections and replace several older interfaces. Standard, Mini, and Micro connector forms preceded Type-C. USB-C is reversible and can carry several protocols and functions, many of which are optional according to the hardware; backward compatibility can reduce signaling rate, offered power, or other capabilities.
