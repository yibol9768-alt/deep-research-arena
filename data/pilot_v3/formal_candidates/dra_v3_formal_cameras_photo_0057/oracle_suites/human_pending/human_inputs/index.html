- a $337.99 Sony A300 body page with no posted review, a 10.2MP APS-C CCD claim and body-based stabilization wording;

- a $1,869 Canon EOS R body bundle with no posted review, a 30.3MP full-frame claim, Dual Pixel AF, 5,655 selectable AF points, an electronic viewfinder and an EF-to-RF adapter.

The available model histories place the D7000 and 7D in 2010 and 2009, and the a7 III, Z6 and EOS R in 2018. General pages discuss back-illuminated sensors, CMOS development, stabilization, electronic viewfinders, rolling shutter and dynamic range. Community discussions include both a recommendation for older prosumer gear and a warning about hidden used-gear problems, a two-year image-quality progression with the same core telescope but changing practice and processing, and a report that a newer phone failed an older phone's close-focus expectation because its lens and mode choices differed. None of this is a matched test of my coworker's physical camera.
