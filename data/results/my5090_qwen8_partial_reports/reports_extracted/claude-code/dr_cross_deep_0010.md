### Issues with Over-Ear Headphones
1. **Physical Comfort**: Over-ear headphones can be bulky and may not fit comfortably with glasses, especially for long flights.
2. **Seal Integrity**: The arms of glasses can break the seal of over-ear headphones, reducing their noise-canceling effectiveness.
3. **Space Constraints**: Over-ear headphones take up more space in a small backpack, which is a concern for travelers.

### Potential Solutions
1. **On-Ear Headphones**: These are generally more compact and may fit better with glasses. They often have a smaller profile, which can be more comfortable for extended wear.
2. **In-Ear Earbuds**: High-quality in-ear earbuds can provide good noise cancellation and are more compact. They are less likely to interfere with the seal of glasses.
3. **Custom-Fit Solutions**: Some manufacturers offer custom-fit earbuds or in-ear monitors that are designed to work with glasses. These can provide a better seal and more comfort.

### Recommendations
- **On-Ear Headphones**: Consider models like the Sony WH-1000XM5 or the Bose QC Ultra. These are compact and offer good noise cancellation.
- **In-Ear Earbuds**: Look for models like the Apple AirPods Pro or the Sony WF-1000XM4. These are compact, offer excellent noise cancellation, and are less likely to interfere with glasses.
- **Custom-Fit Solutions**: If you're open to a more specialized option, consider custom-fit earbuds from brands like Shure or Sennheiser.

### Additional Tips
- **Test Before Buying**: If possible, test different models in-store to see what fits best with your glasses.
- **Check Reviews**: Look for reviews from other frequent flyers who wear glasses to get insights into what works best for them.
