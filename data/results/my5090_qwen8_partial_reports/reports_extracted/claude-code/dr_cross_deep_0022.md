The degradation of phone cases over time is a complex interplay of material science, environmental factors, and usage patterns. Here's a breakdown of why some materials yellow or degrade while others maintain their integrity, and a recommendation for a case that stands the test of time:

### 1. **Material Science: Why Some Cases Yellow or Degradate**
   - **Plastic Cases (Polycarbonate, TPU):**
     - **Yellowing:** Exposure to UV light, heat, and oxidation can cause plastic to yellow over time. This is especially true for cheaper, lower-quality plastics that lack UV stabilizers.
     - **Degradation:** Prolonged use, especially with abrasive materials or frequent exposure to extreme temperatures, can cause the plastic to become brittle or lose its flexibility.
   - **Leather Cases:**
     - **Degradation:** Leather can dry out, crack, and lose its suppleness over time, especially if not properly maintained. This is exacerbated by exposure to moisture, heat, and UV light.
   - **Soft-Touch Cases (Silicone):**
     - **Gumming Up:** Silicone can absorb oils from your skin and become sticky or gummy over time. This is more common in cheaper silicone cases that lack a protective coating.
   - **Metal Cases:**
     - **Corrosion:** Metal cases can corrode if exposed to moisture or salt, especially in humid environments.

### 2. **Why Some Cases Last Longer**
   - **High-Quality Materials:**
     - **Polycarbonate with UV Stabilizers:** These cases are more resistant to yellowing and degradation. They maintain their clarity and strength over time.
     - **TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane):** TPU is more flexible and durable than polycarbonate. It can withstand more wear and tear and is less prone to yellowing.
   - **Composite Materials:**
     - **Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP):** These cases are more rigid and durable, providing better protection while maintaining a sleek appearance.
   - **Leather with Protective Coatings:**
     - **Water-Resistant Leather:** Cases made from water-resistant leather or treated with a protective coating can maintain their suppleness and appearance for longer.

### 4. **Additional Tips**
   - **Regular Cleaning:** Clean your case regularly with a soft cloth to remove oils and dirt that can cause degradation.
   - **Avoid Extreme Temperatures:** Keep your phone and case away from extreme heat or cold to prevent material degradation.
   - **Use a Screen Protector:** A screen protector can help reduce scratches and wear on the case.
