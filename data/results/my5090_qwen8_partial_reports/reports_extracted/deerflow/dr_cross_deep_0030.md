### Impact of Online Requirements on Gaming Experience

- **Offline Play as a Priority**: Local multiplayer games typically require offline play to function properly, as they rely on direct input from all players in the same simulation. This makes them less dependent on servers and more resilient to connectivity issues. Games like *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe* and *Super Smash Bros. Ultimate* are fully playable offline, ensuring a consistent experience regardless of internet availability.

- **Online-Only Games Pose Risks**: In contrast, some modern games, such as *Diablo IV*, require online connections to function, even for single-player or local multiplayer modes. This introduces long-term risks related to server stability and maintenance. If servers are shut down or become unstable, players may lose access to these games, even if they have purchased them.

- **Netcode and Latency**: Online multiplayer games often suffer from input latency and synchronization issues, which can negatively impact fairness and immersion. Local multiplayer games avoid these issues by eliminating network delays, providing a more seamless and responsive experience.
