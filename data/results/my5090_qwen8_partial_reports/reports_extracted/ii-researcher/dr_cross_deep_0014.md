## Key Factors Affecting Keyboard Longevity

The longevity of a keyboard is influenced by several factors, including:

1. **Switch Quality**: Mechanical switches are rated for a certain number of keystrokes, typically 50 million or 100 million. However, the actual lifespan can vary based on the switch type and usage patterns.
2. **Build Quality**: The materials used in the keyboard's construction, such as the PCB (printed circuit board), keycaps, and stabilizers, significantly impact its durability.
3. **User Habits**: Daily use, exposure to liquids, and environmental factors can all affect a keyboard's lifespan.
4. **Brand Reputation**: Established brands often have better quality control and user support, which can contribute to longer-lasting products.

## Evaluation of Switch Durability

Switches are a critical component of a keyboard's longevity. The advertised keystroke ratings (e.g., 50 million or 100 million) are generally based on laboratory testing and may not always reflect real-world usage. However, certain switch types are known for their durability and reliability:

- **Cherry MX Switches**: Cherry MX switches are widely regarded as some of the most durable and reliable mechanical switches. They are rated for up to 50 million keystrokes and are commonly used in high-end mechanical keyboards. Their tactile feedback and linear design make them popular among both gamers and typists.
- **Kailh Switches**: Kailh offers a range of switches, including the Kailh Box and Kailh Choc, which are also rated for up to 50 million keystrokes. These switches are often used in budget-friendly mechanical keyboards and are known for their affordability and durability.
- **Gateron Switches**: Gateron switches are another popular choice for durability. They are rated for up to 50 million keystrokes and are known for their smooth actuation and consistent performance.

While these switches are rated for a certain number of keystrokes, the actual lifespan of a keyboard can be influenced by other factors such as the quality of the PCB and the build materials.
