#### **1. Switch Type: Linear vs. Tactile**
- **Linear switches** (e.g., Cherry MX Red, Kailh Box Red) offer a smooth, consistent keystroke with no tactile bump or click. They are ideal for gaming due to their fast actuation and quiet operation.
- **Tactile switches** (e.g., Cherry MX Brown, Kailh Box Brown) provide a slight bump under your fingers, offering feedback without the click. They are often preferred for typing due to their satisfying feel.

**Critical Insight**: Since you split your time between office work and gaming, **tactile switches** might offer a better balance. They provide feedback for typing while still being fast enough for gaming. However, if you prioritize **gaming performance**, linear switches may be more suitable [1].

#### **2. Build Quality and Durability**
- A **hot-swappable board** allows you to change switches later, which is a **cost-effective** and **flexible** option. It gives you the ability to upgrade your switches without buying a new keyboard.
