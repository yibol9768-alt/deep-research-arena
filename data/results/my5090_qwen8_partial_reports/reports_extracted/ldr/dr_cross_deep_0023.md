### 📱 **Understanding the Difference in Screen Technology**

Flagship phones typically use **OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode)** or **AMOLED (Active-Matrix Organic Light-Emitting Diode)** displays, while budget phones often use **LCD (Liquid Crystal Display)** screens. The key differences between these technologies are:

- **OLED/AMOLED**: Each pixel emits its own light, allowing for **true blacks**, **higher contrast ratios**, **better color accuracy**, and **thinner designs**. These screens are also more energy-efficient in dark environments [1].
- **LCD**: Uses a backlight to illuminate liquid crystals, resulting in **less contrast**, **worse color depth**, and **thicker designs**. LCDs are generally **less power-efficient** in bright environments [2].
