### Understanding the Gap Between Technical Capabilities and User Experience

Budget compact cameras are typically designed for **ease of use, portability, and affordability**, rather than for advanced photography. They often feature **smaller image sensors**, **less sophisticated lenses**, and **limited manual controls** compared to higher-end cameras. These technical limitations can affect image quality, especially in low-light conditions or when capturing fine details [1].

However, for **casual users** like your mom, who is interested in **easy snapshots of her garden and grandkids**, these limitations may not be a concern. The **user experience** is often more about **convenience, reliability, and ease of use** rather than technical specifications. In this context, the **positive reviews** from users who are not professional photographers may be more relevant.

### The Role of User Expectations

Camera enthusiasts often have **higher expectations** regarding image quality, manual controls, and versatility.
