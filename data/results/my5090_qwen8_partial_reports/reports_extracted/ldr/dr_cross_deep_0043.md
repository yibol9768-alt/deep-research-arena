### 🧾 **Loose Leaf vs. Tea Bags: What's the Difference?**

Tea bags are typically made from **tightly compressed tea leaves**, often **lower-grade** leaves that are **broken or cut** during processing. These leaves are designed to **steep quickly** and **release flavor rapidly**, which can result in a **stronger, more intense** cup of tea. However, this can also lead to a **bitter or over-extracted** taste if not brewed carefully.

On the other hand, **loose leaf tea** is usually made from **whole or larger pieces of leaves**, which **steep more slowly** and **release flavor more evenly**. This often results in a **smoother, more nuanced** cup of tea. As you mentioned, if you go through four or five cups a day, the **cost difference** can add up, and **tea bags** may be a more **cost-effective** option for your budget.
