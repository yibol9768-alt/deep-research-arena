A **larger sensor** generally captures more light, which translates to **better performance in low-light environments** [1]. This is because larger sensors have **larger individual pixels**, which can gather more light and produce **less noise** and **better dynamic range** [2]. In a dim gym setting, where lighting can be inconsistent and often not ideal, a larger sensor can make a significant difference in the **clarity and sharpness** of your photos, even when using a slower shutter speed or higher ISO.

While newer cameras often come with **improved features** such as better autofocus, faster burst rates, and more advanced image processing, these benefits may not be as critical for your use case.
