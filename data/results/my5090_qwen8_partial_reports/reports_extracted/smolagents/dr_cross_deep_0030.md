Based on your budget, family needs, and preference for couch gaming, here's a recommendation tailored to your situation:

### Recommended Console: **Nintendo Switch (OLED or Regular Model)**

#### **Why the Nintendo Switch?**
1. **Couch Gaming Experience**: The Switch is designed with couch gaming in mind. It supports local multiplayer out of the box, making it perfect for family game nights with your kids.
2. **Affordability**: The base model (without OLED) is within your budget. The OLED version is slightly more expensive, but if your budget allows, it offers better visuals and a more immersive experience.
3. **Controller Options**: The Switch comes with a Joy-Con controller, and you can purchase an additional Joy-Con or a Pro Controller for $50–$70. This fits your $450 budget comfortably.
4. **Game Library**: The Switch has a strong lineup of family-friendly games, including titles like *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe*, *Super Smash Bros. Ultimate*, *Animal Crossing: New Horizons*, and *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild*. These are excellent for both kids and adults.
5. **Portability**: While you're focused on couch gaming, the Switch's portability is a bonus if you want to play on the go.

#### **Spec Differences and Normal TV**
- **Graphics**: The Switch's graphics are not as advanced as the PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S, but they are more than sufficient for a normal TV. The difference in visual quality is noticeable but not dramatic for casual gaming.
- **Performance**: The Switch is not as powerful as the newer consoles, but it runs games smoothly at 60fps for most titles. It’s more about the variety of games and ease of use than raw power.
- **TV Compatibility**: The Switch works perfectly with a normal TV. You can use the TV as a monitor or even use the Switch in handheld mode if you want to take it outside.

#### **Alternative Option: PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S**
- **Graphics and Performance**: These consoles offer superior graphics and performance, but they are more expensive and may not be as family-friendly for couch gaming.
- **Game Library**: Both have strong game libraries, but they are more geared toward single-player experiences and may not be as suitable for local multiplayer.
- **Budget Consideration**: The PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are significantly more expensive than the Switch, so they may not fit within your $450 budget unless you're willing to stretch it.

### Final Recommendation:
**Choose the Nintendo Switch (regular or OLED model)** with an additional controller. It’s the best fit for your family’s needs, budget, and preference for couch gaming. It offers a great balance of affordability, ease of use, and a wide range of family-friendly games that will keep your kids entertained for a long time.
