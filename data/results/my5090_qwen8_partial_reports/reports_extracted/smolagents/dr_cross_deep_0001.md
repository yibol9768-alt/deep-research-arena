### **Key Considerations:**
- **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)**: Works by using microphones to detect ambient noise and cancel it out. It’s effective in environments like buses and offices.
- **Bluetooth Codecs (e.g., AAC, SBC, aptX)**: These determine the quality of the audio transmission. Higher-end codecs like **aptX** or **LDAC** offer better sound quality, especially with compatible devices.
- **Price vs. Performance**: While more expensive models often offer better ANC, sound quality, and battery life, many mid-range models provide excellent value for the price.

---

### **Final Tip:**
If you're unsure about which brand to choose, look for models with **positive reviews for ANC performance**, **comfort**, and **battery life**. Avoid being swayed solely by brand names—focus on the features that matter most to your use case.
