### **Why Phone Cases Yellow or Degrade Over Time**

1. **Plastic (Polycarbonate or TPU)**:
   - **Yellowing**: Clear or translucent cases made from polycarbonate or TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) can yellow over time due to **UV exposure**, **chemical reactions with sweat or oils**, and **aging of the polymer**. This is more common in clear cases.
   - **Degradation**: TPU can become **gummy or sticky**, especially if exposed to heat or moisture. This is why some users throw out soft-touch cases after a while.

2. **Leather or PU**:
   - **Degradation**: Leather cases can **dry out**, crack, or fall apart at the corners due to **natural aging** and **lack of moisture**. PU (polyurethane) is a synthetic alternative that can also degrade, especially if it’s not of high quality.
   - **Odor and Staining**: Leather can absorb **oils and dirt**, leading to unpleasant smells and stains over time.

3. **Silicone**:
   - **Degradation**: While silicone is generally more durable, **low-quality silicone** can become **gummy or sticky** over time. It can also **yellow** if exposed to UV light or heat.

4. **Aluminum or Metal**:
   - **Durability**: Aluminum cases are highly durable and resistant to yellowing or degradation. They are **lightweight**, **aesthetic**, and **long-lasting**, but may not offer the same level of shock absorption as other materials.
