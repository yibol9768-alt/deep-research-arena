The perceived difference in sound quality between true wireless earbuds often depends on a combination of factors such as driver size, audio codecs (like aptX, AAC, or SBC), bit depth, sampling rate, and overall build quality. While high-end earbuds can offer superior detail, clarity, and bass response, many users report that the difference is subtle or even imperceptible in casual listening scenarios, especially with lower-quality audio content or in noisy environments.

### Key Takeaways:

1. **Price Cutoff Point**:
   - For most casual users, the **$50–$100 range** often provides a noticeable improvement in sound quality compared to sub-$30 earbuds. However, the **$100–$150 range** tends to be where most users start to perceive meaningful differences, especially with better codecs and driver tuning.
   - **Flagship models** (typically $200+), often offer superior audio fidelity, longer battery life, and better noise cancellation, but the improvement might not be worth the cost for casual listeners.

2. **Role of Bluetooth Codecs**:
   - **aptX**, **aptX HD**, and **LDAC** offer better audio quality than the default **SBC** codec, but the benefits are only audible with compatible devices (e.g., phones, computers, or audio players that support these codecs).
   - These codecs are **not always worth the extra cost** unless you're using high-resolution audio or have compatible gear.

3. **Recommendations**:
   - **For casual users**: Models like the **JBL TUNE 220BT** ($30–$50) or **Anker Soundcore Liberty Air 2 Pro** ($90–$120) offer a good balance of price and sound quality.
   - **For audiophiles**: Models like the **Sony WF-1000XM5** ($299) or **Apple AirPods Pro 2** ($249) offer exceptional sound quality, noise cancellation, and codec support, but they are not necessary for casual use.

4. **Personal Experience**:
   - Many users report that the difference between $30 and $280 earbuds is minimal in everyday use, especially with streaming services that compress audio. However, audiophiles or those who listen to high-resolution audio may notice a difference.
