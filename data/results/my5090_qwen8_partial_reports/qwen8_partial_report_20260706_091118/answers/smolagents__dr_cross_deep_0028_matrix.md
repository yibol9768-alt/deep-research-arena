The folklore about cheap phones aging badly is **largely true**, and it's not just "snobs" being overly critical. While many budget phones have excellent initial performance and positive reviews, their long-term reliability and performance can degrade over time. Here's a breakdown of why this happens and what you should consider when choosing a phone for your mom:

---

### **Why Cheap Phones May Age Badly**
1. **Lower-Quality Components**: 
   - Cheap phones often use lower-quality batteries, screens, and internal hardware. These components are more prone to wear and tear over time.
   - For example, cheaper lithium-ion batteries degrade faster than higher-quality ones, leading to shorter battery life.

2. **Limited Software Support**:
   - Budget phones may not receive regular software updates from manufacturers. This can lead to:
     - **Security vulnerabilities**.
     - **Performance issues** (e.g., apps slowing down or crashing).
     - **Inability to support newer apps or features**.

3. **Hardware Limitations**:
   - These phones may have **less RAM**, **slower processors**, or **lower-quality storage**, which can cause slowdowns over time, especially with heavy usage or as the system accumulates data.

4. **Battery Degradation**:
   - Even with good initial reviews, cheap phones often have **lower battery capacity** and **poorer battery management**, leading to faster battery fading.

5. **Build Quality**:
   - Some budget phones use cheaper materials (e.g., plastic frames, lower-quality glass), which may be more prone to scratches, cracks, or other physical damage over time.

---

### **Why Reviews Might Be Optimistic**
- **Short-Term Focus**: Many reviews are based on initial experiences (e.g., first few months), not long-term use. Users may not report issues that arise after a year or two.
- **User Behavior**: Some users may not use their phones to their full capacity (e.g., heavy app usage, frequent updates), which can mask performance issues.
- **Marketing and Promotions**: Manufacturers may highlight the phone's features and performance at launch, but not the long-term reliability.

---

### **What to Look For in a Budget Phone for Your Mom**
If your mom only needs calls, photos, and messaging, you can still find a **reliable budget phone** by focusing on the following:

1. **Choose a Reputable Brand**:
   - Brands like **Samsung**, **Xiaomi**, **Realme**, or **Infinix** often offer budget phones with better build quality and longer software support than lesser-known brands.

2. **Check for Software Updates**:
   - Look for phones that receive **at least 2–3 years of updates**. This ensures your mom’s phone stays secure and functional.

3. **Battery Life and Quality**:
   - Look for phones with **at least 4,000 mAh battery** and **good battery management** (e.g., fast charging, long-term battery health).

4. **Avoid Overly Cheap Models**:
   - While some budget phones are great, the **cheapest models** (e.g., under $100) often have the worst components and support.

5. **Read Long-Term Reviews**:
   - Look for reviews from users who’ve had the phone for **over a year**. These reviews are more likely to reflect real-world performance.
