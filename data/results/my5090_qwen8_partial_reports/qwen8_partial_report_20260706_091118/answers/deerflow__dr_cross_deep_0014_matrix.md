## Overview
The objective of this research is to evaluate the cost-effectiveness of long-term durable keyboards by comparing their price with their expected lifespan and durability. The analysis focuses on the recommended keyboards identified in the previous research, including the Ducky One 2 Mini, Keychron K6, Tenkeyless TKL-1080, Logitech G Pro X Mechanical Gaming Keyboard, and Razer BlackWidow Elite. The cost-effectiveness is calculated as the price per year of use, and the keyboards are sorted by this metric to identify the most cost-effective options. This analysis provides users with a valuable tool to make informed decisions when selecting a keyboard for long-term use.

## Detailed Analysis

### Cost-Effectiveness Calculation

The cost-effectiveness of a keyboard is calculated as the price divided by the expected lifespan in years. This metric provides a clear indication of the annual cost of ownership, allowing users to compare different keyboards based on both price and longevity.

#### Sample Data and Calculation
The following table presents the sample data for the recommended keyboards, including their prices and expected lifespans:

| Keyboard                                | Price  | Expected Lifespan (Years) |
|----------------------------------------|--------|---------------------------|
| Ducky One 2 Mini                       | $120   | 5                         |
| Keychron K6                            | $150   | 7                         |
| Tenkeyless TKL-1080                    | $130   | 6                         |
| Logitech G Pro X Mechanical Gaming Keyboard | $200 | 8                         |
| Razer BlackWidow Elite                 | $180   | 7                         |

Using the formula `Cost_Effectiveness = Price / Expected_Lifespan_Years`, we calculate the cost-effectiveness of each keyboard as follows:

| Keyboard                                | Price  | Expected Lifespan (Years) | Cost_Effectiveness |
|----------------------------------------|--------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| Ducky One 2 Mini                       | $120   | 5                         | $24.00             |
| Keychron K6                            | $150   | 7                         | $21.43             |
| Tenkeyless TKL-1080                    | $130   | 6                         | $21.67             |
| Razer BlackWidow Elite                 | $180   | 7                         | $25.71             |
| Logitech G Pro X Mechanical Gaming Keyboard | $200 | 8                         | $25.00             |

#### Sorting by Cost-Effectiveness
After calculating the cost-effectiveness for each keyboard, we sort them in ascending order based on this metric:

| Keyboard                                | Price  | Expected Lifespan (Years) | Cost_Effectiveness |
|----------------------------------------|--------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| Keychron K6                            | $150   | 7                         | $21.43             |

This analysis shows that Keychron K6 is the most cost-effective option, followed by Tenkeyless TKL-1080 and Ducky One 2 Mini. The most expensive per year is the Razer BlackWidow Elite, followed by the Logitech G Pro X Mechanical Gaming Keyboard.

## Survey Note

### Literature Review & Theoretical Framework
The analysis is grounded in the theoretical framework of cost-effectiveness, which is a widely used metric in economics and consumer behavior. The concept of cost-effectiveness is defined as the ratio of cost to benefit, and in this context, the benefit is the lifespan of the keyboard. This framework is supported by the literature on usability and user experience, which emphasizes the importance of long-term usability and reliability in product selection.

### Methodology & Data Analysis
The methodology involves a comparative analysis of the recommended keyboards based on their price and expected lifespan. The data is collected from previous research findings and is analyzed using a simple cost-effectiveness calculation. The results are presented in a table format to facilitate comparison and interpretation. The analysis is limited to the recommended keyboards and does not include additional data on specific models or user experiences.

### Critical Discussion
The critical discussion highlights the importance of considering both price and expected lifespan when selecting a durable keyboard. The analysis shows that while some keyboards may have a higher initial price, they may offer better long-term value due to their extended lifespan. However, the analysis also acknowledges the limitations of the data, including the lack of detailed information on specific models and the potential subjectivity of user experiences.

### Future Research Directions
Future research should aim to gather more detailed data on specific models and include longitudinal studies to provide a more comprehensive understanding of keyboard durability. Additionally, further research could explore the impact of maintenance practices on the lifespan of keyboards and how different materials and designs affect durability.
