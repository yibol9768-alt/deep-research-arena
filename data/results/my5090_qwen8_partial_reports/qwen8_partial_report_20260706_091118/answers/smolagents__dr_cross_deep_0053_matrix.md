### **Why Sensor Size Matters in Low Light (Dim Gym Environment)**
Sensor size is a critical factor in low-light photography. A **larger sensor** generally captures more light, which translates to:
- **Better noise control**: Larger sensors can capture more light per pixel, reducing noise in low-light conditions.
- **Improved dynamic range**: Larger sensors can handle a wider range of brightness levels, preserving details in both shadows and highlights.
- **Better low-light performance**: This is especially important for indoor volleyball games, where lighting can be dim and inconsistent.

Smaller sensors (like those in compact cameras) often use **higher ISO settings** to compensate for less light, which can introduce more noise and reduce image quality.
