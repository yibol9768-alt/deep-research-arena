### 🔌 **What Makes a Charger Dangerous?**

A charger can be dangerous if it **fails to meet safety standards**, such as those set by the **Underwriters Laboratories (UL)** or the **International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)**. Dangerous chargers may:

- **Overheat** due to poor insulation or substandard materials, potentially causing **melting ports** or **fire hazards** [1].
- **Supply incorrect voltage or current**, which can **damage your phone** or even cause **electrical shocks** [2].
- **Lack proper circuitry** to regulate power, leading to **overcharging** or **short circuits** [3].

These issues are often associated with **low-quality or counterfeit chargers**, which may not be tested for safety or may use **inferior components** [4].

---

### ⚠️ **Why Do Some People Say "Never Buy Off-Brand"?**

The warnings from gadget communities are not unfounded. Many **low-cost chargers** are produced by **unverified manufacturers** and may not adhere to safety standards. These chargers can:

- **Cause long-term damage** to your phone’s battery or internal components [5].
- **Fail to provide consistent power**, leading to **slow charging** or **inconsistent performance** [6].
- **Lack certifications** like **CE**, **FCC**, or **CCC**, which are indicators of safety and quality [7].

These issues are often **not immediately visible**, but they can lead to **serious problems over time**, such as **battery degradation**, **device malfunctions**, or even **safety hazards** [8].

- Some users may not be aware of the **hidden risks** of using low-quality chargers [9].

### 📌 **What Makes a Charger Safe vs. Just Slow?**

A **safe charger** will:

- **Comply with safety standards** (e.g., UL, IEC).
- **Have proper insulation** and **overcurrent protection**.
- **Provide the correct voltage and current** for your device.
- **Be certified** by recognized organizations.

A **slow charger** may:

- **Have lower amperage** (e.g., 1A instead of 2.4A).
- **Be a genuine, but lower-powered** charger.
- **Be safe** but **inefficient** in terms of charging speed.

So, a **slow charger is not inherently dangerous**, but a **dangerous charger is not necessarily slow** [11].
