### **2. Are 'Ergonomic' Controllers Just Marketing?**

While the term **"ergonomic"** is often used as a marketing tool, **genuine ergonomic controllers** are designed with specific anatomical considerations in mind. These include:

- **Wider or more contoured shapes** to accommodate larger hands.
- **Repositioned buttons** to reduce thumb strain.
- **Material choices** that provide better grip and reduce slippage.

However, **not all controllers labeled as ergonomic** are equally effective. It's important to look for **third-party reviews**, **user testimonials**, and **scientific studies** to determine which products are genuinely effective for large hands.
