### Caffeine Content in Decaf Coffee:
Decaffeinated coffee typically contains **2-12 mg of caffeine per 8-ounce cup**, compared to regular coffee, which has **95-200 mg**. The decaffeination process removes most of the caffeine, but a small amount remains. The exact amount can vary depending on the method used (e.g., Swiss Water Process, chemical solvents, etc.), with the Swiss Water Process being considered one of the gentler and more natural methods.

### Naturally Low-Caffeine Teas:
Certain teas are naturally low in caffeine and may be suitable for your restriction:
- **Herbal teas** (e.g., chamomile, peppermint, ginger, valerian root) are naturally caffeine-free.
- **White tea** typically has the lowest caffeine content among teas, with about **15-30 mg per cup**.
- **Green tea** has moderate caffeine, around **20-45 mg per cup**.
- **Black tea** has more caffeine, around **40-70 mg per cup**.
