The trade-off between noise-canceling over-ear headphones and wireless earbuds with a rugged speaker is a nuanced one, and the choice ultimately depends on your priorities and how you plan to use the devices. Let's break down the key factors and then I'll provide a recommendation.

### Noise-Canceling Over-Ear Headphones
**Pros:**
- **Superior Noise Cancellation:** Over-ear headphones can offer more effective noise cancellation because they can completely enclose the ears and use larger drivers to generate the counter-frequency sound waves that cancel out ambient noise.
- **Comfort:** They are generally more comfortable for extended listening sessions, especially for work or long commutes.
- **Sound Quality:** They often provide better sound quality due to larger drivers and more room for sound to resonate.

**Cons:**
- **Portability:** They are bulkier and less convenient for on-the-go use.
- **Price:** High-quality over-ear headphones can be more expensive, which might limit your budget for other items.

### Wireless Earbuds with a Rugged Speaker
**Pros:**
- **Portability:** Earbuds are compact and convenient for on-the-go use, making them ideal for activities like camping.
- **Versatility:** A rugged speaker can be used for both music and voice communication, making it a versatile addition to your gear.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** You can potentially get a decent pair of earbuds and a rugged speaker within the same budget.

**Cons:**
- **Noise Cancellation:** While some high-end earbuds offer decent noise cancellation, they generally can't match the performance of over-ear headphones.
- **Comfort:** Earbuds can be less comfortable for extended use and may not provide the same level of sound isolation.

### Recommendation
Given your budget of $250 and the need to balance between noise cancellation for your office and portability for camping, I would recommend the following:

1. **Noise-Canceling Over-Ear Headphones:** If you prioritize noise cancellation and comfort for your work environment, consider a mid-range over-ear headphone. This would allow you to focus on your work without distractions.

2. **Wireless Earbuds with a Rugged Speaker:** If you value portability and versatility, a pair of decent wireless earbuds paired with a rugged speaker would be a good choice. This setup would allow you to enjoy music and communication on your camping trips while still providing some noise cancellation.
