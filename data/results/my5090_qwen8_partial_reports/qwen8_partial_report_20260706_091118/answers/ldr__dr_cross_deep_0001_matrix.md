### 🎧 **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Bluetooth Codecs**

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) is a feature that uses microphones to detect ambient noise and then produce inverse sound waves to cancel it out. According to [4], the AKG K371, while not an ANC model, was praised for its sound quality during a demo. This suggests that ANC can significantly improve listening experience in noisy environments, but it's not the only factor to consider.

Bluetooth codecs like AAC, SBC, and aptX affect audio quality. While the sources don't directly address this, [10] discusses the importance of audio equipment like DACs (Digital-to-Analog Converters), which can influence how well your audio is decoded and played back. This implies that the quality of the audio signal is important, and while Bluetooth codecs do matter, they are just one part of the audio chain.
