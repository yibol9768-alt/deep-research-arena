# Choosing a Durable Mechanical Keyboard: A Comprehensive Guide for Long-Term Use

When selecting a mechanical keyboard for long-term use, the durability of the components is a critical factor. The user has experienced multiple keyboard failures within two years, with each failure attributed to different issues such as stuck keys, flaky cables, and worn-out keycaps. This report aims to identify mechanical keyboard boards that are known for their longevity and reliability, based on user experiences, technical specifications, and expert recommendations.

## Understanding Mechanical Keyboard Durability

Mechanical keyboards are known for their tactile feedback and durability, but the lifespan of a keyboard can vary significantly depending on the quality of its components. The switches, keycaps, stabilizers, and circuit board are the primary components that can affect the lifespan of a keyboard. Switches are often rated for a certain number of keystrokes, typically ranging from 50 to 100 million. However, these ratings may not always reflect real-world usage, as other components can fail before the switch count is reached ([Mechanical keyboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_keyboard)).

## Evaluating Key Components for Longevity

### Switches

Switches are the heart of a mechanical keyboard, and their durability is often the primary concern for users seeking long-term reliability. Linear switches, such as the Cherry MX Red, are known for their smooth actuation and long lifespan, with ratings up to 50 million keystrokes ([Cherry MX switches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_MX_switches)). However, some users report that the keycaps and stabilizers can wear out faster, especially in high-usage scenarios ([Keycap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycap)).

### Keycaps

Keycaps are another critical component that can affect the lifespan of a keyboard. High-quality keycaps are made from durable materials such as PBT or ABS, which are resistant to wear and tear. PBT keycaps are generally considered more durable than ABS keycaps, making them a preferred choice for long-term use ([Keycap material](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycap_material)).

### Stabilizers

Stabilizers are used to ensure smooth key actuation for larger keys such as the spacebar and Enter key. Poorly designed stabilizers can lead to flaky performance and premature failure. High-quality stabilizers are often made from durable materials and are designed to withstand the rigors of daily use ([Stabilizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizer_(keyboard))) ([Stabilizer design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizer_design)).

### Circuit Board

The circuit board is the backbone of a mechanical keyboard, and its durability can significantly impact the keyboard's lifespan. High-quality circuit boards are built to withstand the rigors of daily use and are often made from durable materials such as FR-4. The circuit board is also responsible for routing signals from the switches to the host computer, and its reliability is crucial for long-term use ([Circuit board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_board)) ([Circuit board durability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_board_durability)).
