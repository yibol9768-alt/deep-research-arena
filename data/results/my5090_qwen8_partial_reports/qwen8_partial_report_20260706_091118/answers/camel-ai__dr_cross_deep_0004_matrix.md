# A Comprehensive Guide to Starting Photography with a $800 Budget

Starting photography can be an exciting journey, but it's important to make informed decisions to ensure you get the best value for your budget. With around $800, you have several options to consider: purchasing a new mirrorless camera with a lens, hunting for a used DSLR kit, or exploring other alternatives. This guide will explore the trade-offs of each option, provide insights from actual photographers, and offer recommendations based on your budget and goals.

## Mirrorless Camera Options

Mirrorless cameras are becoming increasingly popular due to their compact size, advanced features, and excellent image quality. However, they can be more expensive than DSLRs, especially when considering the cost of lenses. Here are some options within your budget:

### Canon EOS 5D Mark IV
The Canon EOS 5D Mark IV is a high-end DSLR that offers exceptional image quality and performance. However, it is quite expensive, with an introductory price of around $3,499. This makes it a less viable option for a $800 budget. [Canon EOS 5D Mark IV](http://localhost:8090/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic/Canon_EOS_5D_Mark_IV)

### Nikon Z-mount
The Nikon Z-mount is a lens mount used in Nikon mirrorless cameras. It is known for its large size and excellent performance, but it is not a camera itself. The Z-mount is a feature that allows for high-quality lenses, but it does not provide a complete camera system. [Nikon Z-mount](http://localhost:8090/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic/Nikon_Z-mount)

### Pentax K-x
The Pentax K-x is a digital single-lens reflex camera that offers a good balance of features and affordability. It is a used DSLR that can be found for under $800, making it a viable option for beginners. The Pentax K-x has a 23.6 × 15.8 mm CMOS image sensor and a maximum resolution of 12.4 megapixels.
