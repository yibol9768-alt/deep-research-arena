Based on your budget and usage, here's a breakdown of what matters and a recommendation for your first mechanical keyboard:

### What Matters at $120:
1. **Switch Type (Linear vs. Tactile):**
   - **Linear Switches** (e.g., Cherry MX Red, Kailh Box Red) are smooth, quiet, and have a consistent actuation force. They are great for long typing sessions and gaming because they don’t have a tactile bump or click.
   - **Tactile Switches** (e.g., Cherry MX Brown, Kailh Box Brown) offer a slight bump when actuated, giving feedback. They are often preferred for typing as they feel more responsive and can reduce fatigue during long writing sessions. However, they may feel less consistent in gaming due to the tactile feedback.

2. **Board Type (Hot-Swappable vs. Prebuilt):**
   - **Hot-Swappable Boards** allow you to change switches later, which is great if you're unsure about your preference or want to experiment with different switch types. They are more flexible but often more expensive upfront.
   - **Prebuilt Boards** are ready to use and often come with a set of switches. They are cheaper upfront but less flexible if you want to upgrade or change switches later.

3. **Build Quality and Durability:**
   - Look for a board with a solid PCB, good keycaps, and a reliable manufacturer. Even at this price, some brands offer decent build quality.

4. **Aesthetic and Layout:**
   - Consider the layout (e.g., TKL, full-size) and RGB lighting if you prefer it. These can be subjective, but they can enhance your experience.

### Recommendation:
Given your usage (office writing and gaming), **tactile switches** may be a better fit because they offer feedback that can help with typing accuracy and reduce fatigue during long writing sessions. However, if you prefer a smoother, quieter feel for gaming, linear switches could be better.

If you're unsure, **a hot-swappable board** is a safer bet at this price point. It allows you to try different switches later without having to buy a new keyboard.

### Suggested Purchase:
- **Board:** A hot-swappable board like the **Kailh V60 Pro** or **Glorious Model 3** (if within budget).
- **Switches:** Start with **Kailh Box Brown** (tactile) or **Kailh Box Red** (linear) for a balanced experience.

This setup allows flexibility and ensures you can find the right feel for both office work and gaming without overspending.
