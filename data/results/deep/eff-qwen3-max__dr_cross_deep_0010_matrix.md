### 2.1 V. Harikrishna: Indian Film Composer and Producer
**V. Harikrishna**, born in 1974 in Bengaluru, India, is a prominent **Kannada film composer**, **playback singer**, and **music producer** [2]. ### 2.2 Gary Wallis: British Session Musician and Musical Director
**Gary Wallis**, born in 1964 in London, is a versatile **drummer, percussionist, keyboardist**, and **musical director** with a career spanning since 1981 [4]. ## 3. Historical and Technological Context

### 3.1 Evolution from Typewriter to Modern Keyboard
The **typewriter**, introduced commercially in 1874, was the dominant text-input device until the 1980s, used widely in offices, education, and by writers [6]. It functioned via mechanical levers striking an inked ribbon to imprint characters on paper [6]. The transition to **electronic keyboards**—first on computers, now refined into mechanical variants—represents a shift from purely mechanical output to digital input with haptic feedback. Modern mechanical keyboards like the Apex Pro inherit the typewriter’s core layout but add layers of electronic intelligence, customization, and multimedia control.

### 3.2 Show Control and Integrated Performance Systems
**Show control** refers to the automation and synchronization of multiple entertainment subsystems—lighting, sound, video, rigging—into a unified performance [8]. This technology is essential in concerts, theater, and large-scale productions. Professionals like **Gary Wallis**, who serve as musical directors for complex acts (e.g., Il Divo, Westlife), likely interact with or oversee such systems [4]. While not directly related to keyboard hardware, show control underscores the broader ecosystem in which modern musicians operate—one reliant on precise timing, digital interfaces, and integrated hardware/software platforms.
